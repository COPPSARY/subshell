# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

SubShell welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful, constructive, patient, and specific. Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.

Positive behavior includes:

- giving actionable technical feedback without personal attacks;
- respecting different viewpoints, identities, and levels of experience;
- keeping disagreements focused on project outcomes and evidence;
- acknowledging mistakes and helping repair harm;
- protecting private information and credentials.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately, and applied consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's contact channel. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential as far as practical.

This code applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community channels, and public representation of SubShell.
